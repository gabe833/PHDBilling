Privacy Policy

PHD Properties · Charleston, SC · Last updated October 2, 2026

PHD Billing is an internal tool used by PHD Properties staff. This page explains what information it handles and how.

Information it handles

  • Staff sign-in: name and email from Google sign-in, used only to decide who can access the App.
  • Property records: properties, owners, tenants' names and contact details, rent, bills, statements and invoices.
  • QuickBooks data: vendors, customers, accounts and items needed to record bills, bill payments, invoices and payments.
  • Payment data: Nickel record IDs and payment status. Bank account and card numbers are entered directly into Nickel and are never stored by the App.

How it is used

Only to run PHD Properties' property management and bookkeeping: paying vendors, billing owners, preparing statements and keeping QuickBooks up to date. Information is never sold or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Records are stored in a Supabase database protected by sign-in and row-level security. QuickBooks and Nickel credentials are kept on the server and never sent to the browser. Every change is logged with who made it and when.

Sharing

Information is shared only with the services that run the App (Google, Supabase, Vercel, Nickel and Intuit QuickBooks) and only as needed to provide it, or when required by law.

Disconnecting QuickBooks

Disconnecting QuickBooks, from the App or from your Intuit account, ends the App's access to your company file. Disconnecting from the App also deletes its stored access tokens. Entries already created in QuickBooks stay in your company file.

Retention and requests

Records are kept as long as needed for PHD Properties' business and legal obligations. To ask about, correct or delete your information, email us.
